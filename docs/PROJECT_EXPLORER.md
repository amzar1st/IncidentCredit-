**One-liner** — Verify regional outage claims and calculate SLA credits from public evidence on GenLayer.

**Overview** — IncidentCredit gives providers and customers an auditable way to resolve regional outage-credit claims. Providers register immutable SLA terms, covered regions and authorized evidence publishers. Customers open claims using commit-pinned reports. GenLayer validators independently fetch the reports, verify SHA-256 digests, and agree on the evidence and regional findings. The contract applies the SLA's minimum duration, exclusions, rate and cap. Either party can challenge, and the dashboard reads finalized state and tracks Studio transaction receipts. In the synthetic StudioNet demo, US East qualified for $15.00 after challenge and re-review, while EU West was denied because it was unaffected. Writes use Studio's built-in account.
